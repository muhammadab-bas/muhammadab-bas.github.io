CV / Courses

An academic foundation in plant sciences

Courses & learning

Coursework completed across foundational plant biology, genetics, biochemistry, and statistical research methods.

Plant Diversity & Systematics

Diversity of Plants

Comparative study of life forms, cellular structures, reproductive mechanisms, and economic importance across viruses, bacteria, algae, fungi, bryophytes, pteridophytes, and gymnosperms

Diversity of Vascular Plants

Detailed comparative study of lower and higher vascular plants (Pteridophytes and Gymnosperms), emphasizing evolutionary lineage, morphology, and anatomy.

Phycology and Bryology

Comprehensive study of classification, morphological organization, reproduction, evolutionary trends, and economic significance of algae and bryophytes.

Biodiversity and Conservation

Explores global and local plant biodiversity patterns, threats to ecosystems, conservation strategies, and sustainable resource management.

Plant Systematics, Anatomy & Development

Covers angiosperm classification systems, taxonomic identification, plant tissue anatomy, organ development, and embryonic development.

Plant Systematics

Focuses on floral morphology, taxonomic identification keys, plant nomenclature, and diagnostic family characteristics of major angiosperm families.

Plant Anatomy

Advanced structural analysis of plant cell types, tissue systems, vascular cambium development, secondary growth, and anatomical adaptations to ecological stress.

Plant Physiology & Biochemistry

Plant Physiology and Ecology

Fundamental course on essential plant physiological processes—including water relations, transpiration, and photosynthetic pathways—and their environmental interactions.

Plant Physiology-I

Detailed examination of plant water relations, stomatal biomechanics, mineral nutrient uptake, phloem translocation, and solute transport.

Plant Physiology-II

Advanced study of plant growth regulators, phytohormone signaling, photo-morphogenesis, seed dormancy mechanisms, and physiological stress responses.

Plant Biochemistry-I

Introduces bioenergetics, photosynthetic light and dark reactions, enzyme kinetics, and the biosynthesis of primary plant carbohydrates and proteins.

Plant Biochemistry-II

Advanced intermediary metabolism covering lipid biosynthesis, nitrogen fixation, cellular respiration, and major secondary metabolite pathways.

Medicinal Plants

Study of therapeutic plant species, crude drug classification, phytochemical extraction, and active bioactive constituents—focusing on local medicinal flora of Pakistan, pharmacological evaluation, and laboratory drug analysis.

Genetics & Molecular Biology

Cell Biology, Genetics, and Evolution

Plant cell organelle ultrastructure, cell division dynamics, Mendelian inheritance principles, gene linkage mapping, and evolutionary mechanisms.

Molecular Biology

Molecular mechanics of DNA replication, transcription, RNA processing, protein synthesis, gene regulation networks, and recombinant DNA tools.

Advanced Molecular Biology

Covers modern genetic transformation, gene editing technologies, transcriptomics, molecular markers, and bioinformatic sequence analysis.

Genetics-I

In-depth study of the physical and chemical basis of heredity, chromosomal structural changes, crossover mechanisms, and point mutations.

Genetics-II

Advanced population genetics, quantitative trait inheritance, cytogenetics, genomic DNA extraction, PCR amplification, and gel electrophoresis.

Ecology & Environment

Plant Ecology-I

Principles of autecology and synecology, soil-plant dynamics, microclimate factors, and quantitative plant community structure analysis.

Plant Ecology-II

Advanced ecosystem energetics, plant population demography, vegetation sampling methodology, ecological succession, and global biome dynamics.

Environmental Biology

Assessment of environmental pollution, climate change impacts on plant flora, habitat degradation, ecotoxicology, and environmental remediation strategies.

Introduction to Environmental Sciences

Interdisciplinary study of Earth's environmental spheres, natural resource utilization, environmental degradation, and sustainability.

Mycology & Pathology

Bacteriology and Virology

Bacterial morphology, viral replication cycles, classification schemes, industrial microbial applications, and plant-microbe interactions.

Mycology and Plant Pathology

Fungal thallus structure, life histories, etiology of major plant diseases, host-pathogen interactions, epidemiology, and disease control.

Applied Mycology

Industrial applications of fungi, commercial mushroom cultivation, fungal enzyme production, biotechnology, and mycoremediation.

Statistics & Research

Mathematics-1 (Algebra)

Foundational algebraic functions, systems of linear equations, quadratic formulas, matrices, determinants, and mathematical modeling.

Introduction to Statistics

Fundamentals of biological data collection, tabular and graphical presentation, central tendency measures, variance, and basic probability theory.

Biostatistics

Applied statistical analysis for biological data, including hypothesis testing, t-tests, ANOVA, Chi-square tests, correlation, and experimental design.

Research Methodology

Scientific research design, literature review techniques, hypothesis formulation, manuscript organization, citation management, and publication ethics.

Undergraduate Research Project / Thesis

A supervised, hypothesis‑driven study in plant stress physiology that involved complete experimental design, controlled laboratory and pot experimentation, quantitative data collection, statistical data analysis, and formal thesis writing and presentation.

Allied & General Sciences

Chemistry-I (Inorganic Chemistry)

Atomic structure principles, chemical bonding theories, periodic properties, acid-base equilibria, and transition metal coordination chemistry.

Chemistry-II (Organic Chemistry)

Structure, reactivity, stereochemistry, synthesis, and functional group properties of primary organic compounds.

Principle of Animal Life-I

Introductory animal biology covering cellular organization, metabolism, tissue structures, and hereditary mechanisms.

Principle of Animal Life-II

Comparative animal organ system physiology, locomotion, reproduction, developmental biology, and homeostatic regulation.

Animal Diversity-I

Taxonomic classification, body plan organization, evolutionary relationships, and ecological adaptations of invertebrate phyla.

Animal Diversity-II

Morphological comparative study, evolutionary adaptations, organ systems, and ecological roles of chordates and vertebrates.

English and Communication Skills

Progressive coursework in functional grammar, spoken communication, academic presentation skills, and technical scientific report writing.
