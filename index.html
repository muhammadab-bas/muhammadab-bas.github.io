Rooted in curiosity. Growing through research.

I’m a BS Botany graduate from the University of Swat, Pakistan, with interests in plant stress physiology, plant molecular biology, and plant health.

My undergraduate research explored proline seed priming and salt-stress tolerance in lentil. That experience strengthened my interest in connecting plant responses with the physiological and biochemical processes behind them.

I am also developing my scientific writing and evidence-synthesis skills, with a growing interest in citrus Huanglongbing (HLB) and plant–microbe interactions. I hope to pursue graduate research that contributes to healthier, more resilient crops.
