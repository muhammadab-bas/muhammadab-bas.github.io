Skills I am building

A thoughtful writing process.

01 / SEARCH

Find the evidence

Build clear questions, use advanced database searches, and keep an organised reading trail.

02 / COMPARE

Read with a question

Separate methods, findings, interpretation, and the practical limits of each study.

03 / SYNTHESISE

Develop an argument

Connect studies logically and explain where evidence agrees, differs, or remains incomplete.

04 / REFINE

Revise with care

Check claims against sources, improve structure, and use feedback to make the writing clearer.
