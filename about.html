About Me

A Botany graduate building a path in plant science and research.

Hi, I’m Abbas.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Botany at the University of Swat, Pakistan, graduating with a CGPA of 4.0/4.0 and a gold medal.

My interests centre on plant physiology, abiotic-stress responses, molecular biology, and plant health. I am particularly interested in how research can help explain plant responses and support crop improvement.

Through undergraduate research, scientific writing, and evidence synthesis, I am developing the skills needed for graduate study. My longer-term goal is to contribute to research and teaching that supports climate-resilient agriculture.

My growing interest in citrus Huanglongbing also connects plant physiology with pathology, host–pathogen interactions, and disease management.
