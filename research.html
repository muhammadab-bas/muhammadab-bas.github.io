Undergraduate thesis

Proline seed priming & salt-stress tolerance in lentil

Project: Effects of Proline Seed Priming on Salt Stress Tolerance of Wild and Cultivated Lentil (Lens culinaris Medik.) Genotypes.

Focus: Exploring germination, root traits, and biochemical responses of wild and cultivated lentil genotypes under salinity.

My experience: Seed priming, plant-response measurements, root-image analysis, biochemical assays, and statistical interpretation.

Supervisor: Dr. Ahmad Ali · University of Swat

Evidence-synthesis focus

Citrus Huanglongbing & disease management

I am interested in the biology and management of citrus Huanglongbing, including plant responses, host–pathogen interactions, and treatment evidence.

My evidence-synthesis work focuses on oxytetracycline trunk injection compared with non-oxytetracycline controls in HLB-affected citrus.

Areas of interest include study design, disease-related outcomes, plant performance, and how to interpret evidence across field studies.

Scientific writing

Building research communication skills.

My scientific writing experience includes training at the Multiomics Academy of Research and Science (MARS), literature searching, review writing, and evidence synthesis.

I am developing my ability to connect biological questions with clear scientific argumentation and careful interpretation of the literature.
