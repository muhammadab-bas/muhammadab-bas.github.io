About Me

I am a botany graduate from Swat, Pakistan, with an interest in how plants respond to stress, disease, and environmental change. My work is centered on resilience, function, and practical research for more sustainable crop systems.

Academic path

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Botany at the University of Swat, graduating with distinction as a gold medalist. My research interests include plant stress physiology, plant pathology, molecular genetics, and crop resilience.

My undergraduate thesis focused on the effects of proline seed priming on salt-stress tolerance in wild and cultivated lentils. This work shaped my interest in early stress responses, biochemical markers, and practical strategies for improving plant performance under stress.

I am also developing a systematic review and meta-analysis on trunk-injected oxytetracycline for citrus Huanglongbing, which has strengthened my interest in evidence synthesis and plant health.
